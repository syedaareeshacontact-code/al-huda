Anxiety, worry and emotional pressure can affect anyone.

A person may feel overwhelmed by uncertainty, family problems, financial strain, illness, loneliness or responsibilities that seem too difficult to manage. Experiencing these feelings does not automatically mean that a person has weak faith.

Islam teaches believers to turn to Allah in times of distress. It also encourages responsible action, seeking support and using the lawful means available to address a problem.

This guide shares an authentic prophetic dua for worry and grief, Quranic verses that offer spiritual comfort, and practical steps that may support emotional wellbeing.

It does not present dua as an instant cure or a replacement for professional medical or psychological care.

## An authentic dua for anxiety, worry and grief

Sahih al-Bukhari records that the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ regularly sought Allah's protection from several emotional, personal and financial burdens.

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">
  اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ، وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ
</p>

### Transliteration

**Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan, wal-'ajzi wal-kasal, wal-jubni wal-bukhl, wa dala'id-dayni wa ghalabatir-rijal.**

### Meaning

**O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief, from weakness and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, from the burden of debt and from being overpowered by people.**

This dua is powerful because it does not mention emotional pain alone. It also asks for protection from conditions that may make distress worse.

These include:

* Worry about what may happen
* Grief over what has already happened
* Feeling unable to take action
* Falling into harmful inactivity
* Fear that prevents responsible decisions
* Financial pressure and debt
* Being controlled or overpowered by others

The dua teaches a believer to ask Allah for help with both internal struggles and external pressures.

Remembering Allah may include:

* Making dua
* Performing salah
* Reading or listening to the Quran
* Reciting authentic adhkar
* Reflecting on Allah's names and attributes
* Asking Allah for forgiveness and guidance

These acts can provide spiritual grounding and help a believer feel less alone during hardship.

However, the verse should not be used to claim that every medical anxiety condition will disappear through recitation alone.

A person may sincerely remember Allah and still experience severe anxiety, panic, grief or depression. Seeking treatment in such a situation does not contradict faith or reliance upon Allah.

## How to recite this dua thoughtfully

### Understand the words

Try to learn what each part of the dua means.

When the words are understood, the dua becomes more than a memorised sentence. It becomes an honest conversation with Allah about fear, grief, weakness, pressure and the need for help.

### Recite it sincerely

You may include this dua in your normal personal supplications and regular remembrance of Allah.

The narration in Sahih al-Bukhari 6369 does not specify a fixed number of repetitions. Therefore, this article does not recommend an invented count such as seven, forty or one hundred times.

Recite it sincerely and regularly without treating it as a guaranteed formula.

### Connect dua with practical action

After making dua, ask yourself:

**What safe and responsible action can I take next?**

Depending on the situation, the next step may be:

* Speaking honestly with a trusted family member
* Discussing a financial repayment plan
* Resting after prolonged exhaustion
* Reducing an avoidable source of stress
* Setting a boundary with someone causing harm
* Contacting a doctor or mental-health professional
* Asking an imam or scholar for religious guidance

Making dua and taking practical action can support one another.

Reliance upon Allah does not require a person to ignore the available means of help.

## A simple calming routine

The following routine is practical wellbeing guidance. It is not a narrated religious ritual and does not replace medical treatment.

1. Move to a quiet and safe place when possible.
2. Sit comfortably and slow your breathing without forcing it.
3. Recite the prophetic dua while thinking about its meaning.
4. Identify the immediate concern affecting you.
5. Choose one small and safe next action.
6. Contact a trusted person when you need support.

For example, instead of trying to solve every problem at once, focus on one manageable action such as sending an important message, making an appointment or asking someone to stay with you.

## Quran verses for hope during difficult times

### Ease accompanies hardship

Surah Ash-Sharh repeats the reassurance that ease accompanies hardship.

This does not mean that every difficulty will end immediately or according to the timeline a person expects. It offers hope that hardship is not the whole story and that Allah's wisdom, mercy and help remain present.

### Asking Allah for mercy and relief

The final verse of Surah Al-Baqarah contains a Quranic supplication asking Allah for pardon, forgiveness, mercy and protection from unbearable burdens.

The verse is best read and understood in full rather than reduced to a slogan.

It reminds believers that they may openly ask Allah for help when they feel weak or overwhelmed.

## Anxiety does not always mean weak faith

Emotional distress can have many causes.

These may include:

* Bereavement or personal loss
* Family conflict
* Financial insecurity
* Work or academic pressure
* Physical illness
* Trauma
* Sleep deprivation
* Hormonal or neurological factors
* A diagnosable mental-health condition

It is unfair and potentially harmful to tell every anxious person that they simply need stronger faith.

Spiritual practices may form an important part of care, but a person may also need rest, social support, medical assessment, therapy or other professional treatment.

Compassion is more helpful than blame.

## When should you seek professional help?

Consider contacting a licensed mental-health professional or doctor when anxiety:

* Continues for a long period
* Causes frequent panic attacks
* Seriously affects sleep
* Prevents you from working or studying
* Makes ordinary responsibilities difficult
* Causes you to avoid people or important places
* Produces severe physical symptoms
* Becomes difficult to manage alone

A qualified clinician can assess whether the symptoms relate to anxiety, another health condition or a combination of factors.

A trusted imam or Islamic scholar may support the spiritual and religious side of the situation, but should not be expected to replace a trained medical professional.

Both forms of support may be valuable.

## What to do during an immediate crisis

If you believe you may harm yourself or another person, or you cannot remain safe, seek urgent help immediately.

Contact local emergency services, go to the nearest emergency department, and tell a trusted person who can stay with you.

Do not remain alone while waiting for an article, message or online response to solve the crisis.

## Claims this article does not make

This guide does not claim that:

* The dua guarantees immediate relief
* A fixed number of repetitions must be followed
* Anxiety always comes from weak faith
* Professional treatment is unnecessary
* Medication or therapy should be stopped
* Every anxious feeling represents a medical disorder
* One dua will solve every underlying problem

The dua is an authentic act of worship and a source of spiritual support. It should be used honestly and responsibly.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the authentic dua for anxiety and worry?

The dua recorded in Sahih al-Bukhari 6369 begins:

**Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan...**

It asks Allah for protection from worry, grief, weakness, laziness, cowardice, miserliness, debt and being overpowered by others.

### How many times should I recite this dua?

The cited narration does not give a fixed repetition count. You may recite it sincerely during your normal supplications without attaching an unsupported number to it.

### Does this dua guarantee that anxiety will disappear?

No authentic source cited here promises immediate or guaranteed relief from all anxiety symptoms.

Dua can provide spiritual support, while some people may also need practical assistance or professional treatment.

### Can I seek therapy while relying on Allah?

Yes. Seeking lawful and appropriate treatment does not contradict reliance upon Allah.

A person may make dua, perform worship and receive professional healthcare at the same time.

### Does anxiety mean that my faith is weak?

Not necessarily. Anxiety may arise from emotional, social, physical or medical causes. A person should be supported with compassion rather than blamed.

### Which Quran verse mentions peace through remembering Allah?

Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:28 states that believing hearts find reassurance in the remembrance of Allah.

## Final reflection

The prophetic dua for worry and grief gives believers words to use when their own words feel difficult to find.

It acknowledges fear, sadness, weakness, financial pressure and the feeling of being overwhelmed. It teaches a person to bring these burdens before Allah rather than hiding them.

At the same time, Islam does not require someone to face serious distress alone.

Make dua. Remember Allah. Speak to people you trust. Take the next responsible step. Seek professional care when it is needed.

Spiritual support and practical help do not have to compete with one another.
